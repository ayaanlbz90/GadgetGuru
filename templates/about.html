OUR STORY

Tech that keeps up with real life.

GadgetGuru started as a simple idea shared by a few friends: buying good tech shouldn't mean overpaying, waiting weeks, or guessing if a product is even genuine. So we built a store that keeps it simple — real products, fair prices, and a team that actually replies when you message.

What We Provide

Curated Gadgets

Earbuds, smartwatches, chargers, cameras and more — every product on GadgetGuru is picked and tested for real everyday use, not just specs on a page.

Instant UPI Checkout

Scan, pay, done. No lengthy card forms or third-party redirects — just a fast, secure UPI payment straight from your favorite app.

Honest Order Tracking

Every order gets a proper digital receipt and a status you can actually check — Processing, Shipped, or Delivered — no guessing games.

A Human on WhatsApp

Got a question before you buy, or need help after? Tap the WhatsApp button on any page — you're talking to our real support team, not a bot.

What We're Solving

The Problem

Buying gadgets online often means unclear sellers, slow refunds, and no one to actually talk to when something goes wrong.

Our Fix

A small, accountable team behind every order — real contact details, real WhatsApp support, and a clear payment & delivery trail from checkout to your doorstep.
